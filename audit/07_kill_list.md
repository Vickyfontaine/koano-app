# 07. Kill List

Features, pages, copy, tiers and ideas that dilute KOANO and should be removed, demoted or stopped. Ranked by impact on enterprise value. Each item: what to kill, why, evidence, effort (S/M/L/XL), impact (1 to 10), and who championed it. "Kill" means remove, or stop shipping until a stated condition is met. Locked decisions are untouched: the font, the blue logo, provenance honesty and deterministic math all stay.

The rule used to rank: anything that a sophisticated buyer can catch in five minutes and that costs trust is killed first, even if it is small. KOANO sells honesty, so every false statement it ships costs more than the same statement costs a competitor.

## Ideas to kill (not code)

| # | Idea | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 31 | "More agents" as the path to quality | The AI panel traced that the LLM sets every vote. Splitting reasoning across more blind agents loses cross-domain facts (the Foundation permit next to "97% unused FAR"). Quality comes from deterministic lens scorecards plus an adversarial critic, not more voters. |
| 32 | "Temperature 0 makes it deterministic" | Sonnet 4.6 accepts temperature but wobbles anyway. Every Claude model from 4.7 onward rejects non-default temperature with HTTP 400, and Sonnet 4.6 can be retired from February 17, 2027 ([Anthropic model deprecations](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/model-deprecations)). Determinism must come from code, not sampling settings. |
| 33 | "The archive is the moat" as currently scoped | It is a good harness pointed at mostly reconstructable data. The moat is point-in-time capture of the fields the city overwrites, plus the scored verdict ledger. |
| 34 | Competing with Diald, Built AI and Henry on "AI writes your memo faster" | That is where funded rivals converged in 2025 and 2026. KOANO wins on verifiable, scored and replayable judgment, not on speed of prose. |
| 35 | Selling Portfolio self-serve | No SSO, no SOC 2, no org tenancy, and the IC memo has placeholder sections. Run 3 to 5 paid pilots instead. |
